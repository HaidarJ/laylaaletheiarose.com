Why people come

Look beneath the surface

You may be navigating a crossroads, noticing a recurring pattern or trying to understand a situation that feels difficult to untangle.

A reading offers space to pause and examine your question from another perspective. Together, we can explore:

  • What needs your attention now.

  • A choice between different paths.

  • Patterns in relationships, work or personal life.

  • What may be supporting or obstructing your next step.

Rooted in Ma’at

A considered approach to divination

You can expect a thoughtful reading, direct language and room for your own discernment. I take the work seriously, including its uncertainties.

My practice is rooted in Ma’at: truth, balance and responsibility.

I work with a range of tarot and oracle systems, choosing the tools and layouts according to the question. Where useful, I bring different systems together to explore another angle, examine a recurring theme or question my initial interpretation.
